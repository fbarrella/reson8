# 🎧 Reson8 v1.2.0 Release Notes

This release rounds out the voice and text experience with per-user voice controls, deeper channel management, custom emoji, message editing/deletion, unread indicators, and a brand-new way to grab someone's attention — plus a handful of stability and polish fixes.

---

## ✨ New Features & Improvements

- 🎚️ **Per-User Volume & Local Mute** — Right-click anyone in a voice channel to adjust their volume from 0–200% or mute them locally, just for you. Your preferences are saved per-user and applied automatically the next time they join.
- 🔇 **Self Mute/Deafen Indicators** — See at a glance when someone else has muted their mic or deafened themselves, via small icons next to their name in the channel tree — no more wondering if you're being ignored.
- ✏️ **Channel Rename** — Admins can rename a channel after creation without losing its message history or having to delete and recreate it.
- ↕️ **Drag & Drop Channel Reordering** — Reorder channels within a category simply by dragging them into place.
- 🔞 **NSFW Channels** — Mark a text channel as NSFW; members see a confirmation prompt before entering it.
- 😀 **Custom Emoji Uploads** — Upload your own emoji with a built-in crop/zoom tool. New uploads are queued for admin approval and broadcast live to everyone the moment they're approved.
- ✅ **178 New Default Emojis** — Filled real gaps in the built-in curated set (😉 winking face, 🤦 facepalm, 🤷 shrug, several country flags, and more) — now 550+ emojis in total, up from ~374.
- 🗑️ **Delete Your Own Messages** — Remove a sent message — including its attachment — from a text channel or DM.
- 📝 **Edit Your Own Messages** — Fix a typo or add missing info within 2 minutes of sending a text-channel message; edited messages are clearly marked.
- 📎 **Instant Upload Feedback** — Picking or pasting an image now shows a live thumbnail and progress spinner immediately, with a one-click retry if the upload fails.
- 🔵 **Unread Channel Indicators** — Text channels with messages you haven't seen show a highlighted dot until you open them.
- 👋 **Nudge** — Get a user's attention with a sound, an on-screen toast, and a taskbar/dock flash. Server admins can enable or disable nudging server-wide, and a 30-second per-target cooldown keeps it from being spammy.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Shortcut Sound Alerts** — Muting, deafening, and disconnecting from voice via a keyboard shortcut now plays the same sound cues as clicking the corresponding button (previously silent).
- **Voice Session Timer Contrast** — The elapsed-time timer on active voice channels is now clearly legible against the dark theme, in both the channel tree and the voice panel.
- **Unread Indicators Reappearing** — Fixed a bug where a text channel's unread notification could resurface right after being read, triggered by simply joining or leaving a voice channel.
- **Custom Emoji Upload Not Working** — Fixed the emoji uploader silently doing nothing when selecting an image, caused by a Content-Security-Policy gap that blocked the crop tool's image preview from loading.
- **Settings Modal Flicker** — Non-admin users no longer see a brief flash of admin-only Settings tabs (Roles, Emojis, Server) before they're correctly hidden — the client now already knows a user's permissions by the time Settings is opened.
