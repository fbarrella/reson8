# 🎧 Reson8 v3.0.0-beta.5 Release Notes (Beta)

The fifth beta polishes the new interface: a voice card that is always there, a calmer channel list with avatars, a glass Settings menu, and streams that show up ready to watch the moment someone starts sharing. Earlier in this beta: [beta.1](v3.0.0-beta.1.md), [beta.2](v3.0.0-beta.2.md), [beta.3](v3.0.0-beta.3.md), [beta.4](v3.0.0-beta.4.md).

> [!WARNING]
> This is a beta. Install it only if you want to test the new interface.
> Beta builds update to newer betas automatically; stable builds never see
> them. To go back to stable, install 2.7.x from the Releases page.

> [!NOTE]
> Avatars in the channel list and stream thumbnails need the matching server. An older server keeps working: people show the avatar the app already knows (or the default one) and streams show an avatar instead of a frozen frame.

---

## ✨ New Features & Improvements

- 🟢 **A Voice Card That's Always There** — The voice card now sits under the channel list as its own card. In a room it is green ("Voice connected", the room, your latency and all the buttons); outside a room it is yellow ("Disconnected") with just Mute and Deafen and their device menus. The two versions fade into each other, and the card is the same height in both.
- 🎤 **Mute and Deafen Before You Join** — The buttons work even when you're not in a room. Join muted or deafened and nothing is sent before it takes effect; your choice is remembered when you leave, get kicked or restart the app.
- 🌳 **A Balanced Channel Tree** — Roomier rows, a speaker icon for voice rooms, a coloured square with initials for text channels that have no icon (it keeps its colour when renamed), a lit bar and tint for the channel or room you're in, and tidier timers, counts and tags.
- 🧑 **Avatars With a Speaking Ring** — People in a voice room show their round avatar in the channel list, and a green ring glows around it while they talk. It works even for people who haven't written a message yet.
- 🔮 **Glass Settings Menu** — The category menu in both Settings dialogs is frosted purple glass over a soft glow, with a lit right edge.
- 🖼️ **Ready-to-Watch Streams** — When someone starts sharing, an open voice room tab switches to stream mode on its own: a frozen frame of their screen (or their avatar until it arrives), "{name} is sharing" and a **Watch stream** button. Nothing is received until you press it. A separate window opens only through Pop out.
- 📸 **Stream Thumbnails** — Sharers send a small picture of their screen to the room two seconds after starting and every two minutes after that; it is kept in memory only and shows on the "Also live" cards too.
- 🔴 **You're Sharing** — If you are the one sharing, the stream area shows your own screen locally with a **Stop sharing** button.
- 🏷️ **LIVE Badges Open the Room First** — Clicking a LIVE badge opens the voice room on that stream, then asks "Watch {name}'s stream?". Cancel keeps you on the ready stage.
- 🐧 **Linux Window Buttons** — On KDE the minimize / maximize / close buttons no longer draw a grey block over the app, over dialogs or the image viewer.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- 👢 **Kicks Stick** — A kicked person used to reappear in the room about ten seconds later because the app rejoined by itself. Now a kick ends their voice session for good; they can click the room to join again.
- 🔤 **Long Text Wraps** — A message with a very long unbroken line (a long link, ASCII art, a very long name) no longer makes the whole chat scroll sideways.
- 💬 **Reply Composer Corners** — Under the reply bar the message box now joins it with no gap at the corners, with picked images in between too.
- 🔍 **Image Viewer Fit** — Images fit below the viewer's buttons instead of sliding under them.
- 🐧 **Rounded Corners on Linux** — Dialogs and the image viewer follow the window's rounded corners instead of painting square ones over them.
- 🪟 **Glass Menu Corners** — The new glass menu stays inside the Settings dialog's rounded corners.

---

## ⚠️ Known Issues

- On Linux the window buttons still use the system's own style; they are now transparent, but their look comes from your desktop theme.
- The Linux (Wayland) screen-share picker's thumbnails could not be tested without the system picker; if a portal share shows no frozen frame, please report it.
- The small headings in the Settings menu are a little dim over the glass; this follows the design and may be tuned.
