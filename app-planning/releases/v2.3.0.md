# 🎧 Reson8 v2.3.0 Release Notes

This release is a big pass on text chat and voice quality-of-life: chat history now loads on demand instead of all at once, channels can finally be reorganized and customized after creation, and voice gets a new self-monitoring tool plus several accuracy and reliability fixes.

---

## ✨ New Features & Improvements

- 📜 **Paginated Chat History** — Channels and DMs now load the latest 20 messages at a time, seamlessly fetching more as you scroll up, instead of loading the entire history in one shot every time a tab opens.
- ⬇️ **"Jump to Most Recent Message" Button** — A floating button appears whenever you've scrolled away from the latest message, jumping you straight back down with one click.
- 📁 **Move Channels Between Parents** — A new "Move to…" option on any channel's right-click menu lets admins reorganize the channel tree after creation, instead of only being able to delete and recreate a channel to reposition it.
- ✏️ **Rename Parent/Category Channels** — Renaming (and deleting) a channel is no longer blocked once it has children and becomes a category.
- 🖼️ **Custom Text Channel Icons** — Give any text channel its own icon in the channel tree — pick from the full emoji set, or upload and crop your own image.
- 🎧 **Hear Yourself** — A new toggle in Voice & Shortcuts lets you monitor your own mic in real time with every live adjustment (noise gate, volume, noise cancelling) applied, so you can judge how you actually sound. Automatically mutes and deafens you while active if you're in a voice channel, so you never broadcast while previewing.
- 🎚️ **Noise Cancelling Strength Slider** — Tune how aggressively AI noise cancelling suppresses background sound, instead of it being fixed at maximum strength.
- 🧹 **Reorganized Voice & Shortcuts Tab** — Related controls are grouped more logically, and every switch-plus-slider feature (Hear Yourself, Noise Gate, Noise Cancelling) now expands from one unified card instead of rendering as two visually disconnected boxes.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- 📍 **Chats Opening at a Stale Scroll Position** — Channels and DMs now reliably open scrolled all the way down to the latest message, instead of sometimes landing partway up the history.
- 🔔 **Missing DM "Wake Up" Sound Alert** — Fixed the notification sound intermittently failing to play when a new or reopened DM conversation arrived while the window was focused on something else.
- 📏 **Settings Modal Resizing Per Tab** — The Settings window now keeps a fixed height across every tab instead of growing or shrinking each time you switch tabs.
- 🎙️ **Noise Cancelling Fading Your Voice** — AI noise cancelling no longer fades a quiet or paused voice toward silence — suppression now defaults to a more moderate strength (adjustable via the new slider above) instead of the maximum.
- ⚡ **Delayed Own-Voice Speaking Indicator** — Your own "speaking" halo now lights up instantly instead of waiting on the same server round-trip used for other participants' indicators.
- 🔗 **Broken Rich Link Previews on Bot-Protected Sites** — Links from sites that block simple automated requests now fall back to a real hidden browser view to fetch a preview, fixing previews that silently failed before.
