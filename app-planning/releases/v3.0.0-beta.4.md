# 🎧 Reson8 v3.0.0-beta.4 Release Notes (Beta)

The fourth beta finishes the parts of the new design that earlier betas left out: the voice dock and room controls, a real Members and Bans screen, emoji management, message search, a typing indicator and window buttons that no longer cover the app. Earlier in this beta: [beta.1](v3.0.0-beta.1.md), [beta.2](v3.0.0-beta.2.md), [beta.3](v3.0.0-beta.3.md).

> [!WARNING]
> This is a beta. Install it only if you want to test the new interface.
> Beta builds update to newer betas automatically; stable builds never see
> them. To go back to stable, install 2.7.x from the Releases page.

> [!NOTE]
> Members' "Last seen", "Banned by", emoji removal, typing and search need the matching server (it applies two database migrations on start). An older server keeps working; those parts simply stay empty or hidden.

---

## ✨ New Features & Improvements

- 🎙️ **Voice Dock** — One card with "Voice connected", the room and its category, your latency, and split mic and headset buttons whose arrows switch the input or output device on the spot.
- 🎛️ **Voice Room Header and Call Bar** — The room shows its category, an Encrypted / Not encrypted tag, the people count and a centred call bar (mic, headset, Share screen, Leave) that stays in step with the dock.
- 🏷️ **Server Pill Shows You** — The title bar shows the server's address and your nickname; its menu opens centred under it, at least as wide as the pill, and a second click closes it.
- 👥 **Members Screen** — Search by name or ID, filter by All / Admins / No key / No access, see who is online (and in which room, or when they were last seen), and toggle Member and Admin with one click. The ⋮ menu has Reset key, Identity log, other roles and Ban / Unban.
- 🛡️ **Safer Role Changes** — The server now refuses removing your own admin or Member role, removing the last admin, a non-admin granting admin, and a role from another server.
- 🚫 **Banned People List** — The Bans page lists who is banned, who banned them and when, with the linked address (masked for moderators who aren't admins) and an Unban button; banned addresses use the same card style.
- 😀 **Emoji Requests and Library** — "Approve all", clearer request rows with a count in the menu, and an "On this server" grid where an admin can remove an emoji (it disappears from everyone's picker at once).
- 🔢 **Counts in the Settings Menu** — Members and Emoji show how many there are.
- 📶 **Saved Servers Show Latency** — Each saved server on the welcome screen shows its ping, or "Offline".
- 🖥️ **New Share Picker** — Screens and Windows tabs in one grid, the app's process name under each window (Windows), a Share audio card and an "Others will see" line.
- 🐧 **Linux Screen Picker Flow** — A waiting card while the system picker is open, and "Name your stream" now shows the chosen source with Change, Cancel and Start sharing.
- #️⃣ **Channel Icon When Creating** — Pick an emoji or upload an image for a new text channel right in Create channel.
- ⌨️ **Typing Indicator** — "Name is typing…" under the composer for the channel or DM you are in. Turn off sending yours in Privacy & safety.
- 🔍 **Message Search** — A search field in the tab bar (Ctrl/Cmd+F) with a results column, All channels or this channel/DM, highlighted matches, and a click that jumps to the message.
- 🧪 **Get Beta Updates** — Stable builds can opt in to betas in Settings › About; beta builds show the switch on.
- 🪟 **Window Buttons Stay Out of the Way** — They are transparent like in Discord, dialogs sit below them with an even margin, and the image viewer's controls form one row under them with an opaque Close button.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- 🔊 **Sound Alerts Switch** — The setting now reads "Play sound alerts" (on = sounds), dims the alert volume when off, and nudges still play with alerts off.
- 🗂️ **Minimize to Tray** — Removed a handler that never ran; the window is hidden after minimizing and always restored by the same path.
- 🎚️ **Applying Both Audio Devices** — Saving a new microphone and speaker together in Settings now applies both.
- 🐧 **Linux Window Border and Corners** — On Linux the window had square corners and no border (Windows draws both itself). The app now draws a 1 px border and rounded corners, hidden while maximized or fullscreen.
- 📐 **Dialog Spacing** — The Emoji and Bans pages have the same space under their section titles as every other settings page.
