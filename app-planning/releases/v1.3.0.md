# 🎧 Reson8 v1.3.0 Release Notes

This release introduces automatic updates, a new Audio settings tab for finer volume control, and a proper mute/deafen state machine — plus three targeted bug fixes.

---

## ✨ New Features & Improvements

- 🔄 **Auto-Updates** — Reson8 now checks for new releases automatically on launch and lets you install them with one click from a simple "Update Now" prompt. You can also trigger a check anytime from the new **About** tab in Settings, which shows your current version and a "Check for Updates" button.
- 🔊 **Audio Settings Tab** — Fine-tune your experience with three new volume sliders: **Nudge Volume**, **Sound Alerts Volume**, and a master **Global Voice Chat Volume** that layers on top of each participant's individual volume (e.g. a participant at 150% personal volume with the global slider at 50% comes through at 75% effective volume).
- 🎧 **Mute & Deafen Now Work Together** — Deafening yourself automatically mutes your mic too, since you can't be heard while you can't hear. Un-deafening correctly restores whatever mute state you had beforehand — if you were already muted before deafening, you stay muted after; if you weren't, your mic reopens. Push-to-talk is also now fully blocked while deafened, and clicking Mute while deafened un-deafens you and applies the mute toggle in one action.

---

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Voice Session Timer Blink** — Fixed the voice channel's elapsed-time timer briefly flashing blank every time you toggled mute or deafen.
- **False "Unread" on Your Own Messages** — A text channel no longer shows as unread after you reconnect if the last message in it was one you sent yourself.
- **Linux Taskbar Icon (Wayland)** — Fixed the AppImage showing a generic icon instead of the Reson8 logo in the title bar and app switcher under Wayland desktops.
