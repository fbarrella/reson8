Feature/Fix: Remove the dead will-minimize handler; one show path (PRD 20 H1)

  with `(mainWindow as any).on("will-minimize", …)` never ran and its comment
  described behaviour that does not exist.

  handler: with minimize-to-tray on, the window is minimized and then hidden,

  tray Restore, viewer-return); the one direct show() is the Viewer window's

  `will-minimize` (window-chrome.ts header comment, root AGENTS.md).
